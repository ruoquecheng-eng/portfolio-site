# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are admissions reviewers for taught master's programs in Hong Kong and Singapore. Secondary users are engineering recruiters and technical interviewers. They usually scan on a laptop, under time pressure, and need to distinguish verified work from ambition quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present a truthful technical and academic record across communication engineering, network diagnostics, engineering software, mathematical modeling, and mathematical research. Success means a reviewer can identify the candidate's direction in 10 seconds, understand two substantial projects and two research lines within a few minutes, and trace important claims to evidence or clearly stated limits.

## Brand Personality

Calibrated, rigorous, composed. The voice is direct and technical without sounding promotional. It should create confidence through specificity, readable evidence, and visible limits.

## Anti-references

This must not resemble a cyberpunk developer template, a university marketing page, a dream-school list, a generic startup landing page, or a skill-logo wall. Avoid fake terminals, code rain, hacker aesthetics, neon overload, custom cursors, heavy parallax, particle fields, glassmorphism, glowing borders, and meaningless animation.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before decoration. Real screenshots, figures, repository references, and manuscript facts carry the page.
2. Scope every claim. Simulation, internal validation, submission status, and individual responsibility stay visible.
3. Reveal the system. Architecture, diagnostic flow, modeling workflow, and limitations explain how the work was done.
4. Prioritize the strongest work. NetSage, battery modeling, and research lead; supporting projects remain concise.
5. Make scrutiny comfortable. High contrast, semantic structure, keyboard access, print support, and restrained motion are part of credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, focus, and graphical objects. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, color-redundant status communication, responsive layouts from 360 px upward, descriptive alt text, and a print-ready resume.
